05 / QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Is AuditLens commercially available today?

Not yet. It is in pre-commercial development and evaluation; demonstrations are planned subject to readiness.

Does AuditLens connect to Waters Empower?

Read-only Empower integration is planned. It is not currently represented as a verified, production-ready connector.

Is AuditLens FDA approved or validated?

No FDA approval or validation claim is made. Product testing and formal validation work remain to be completed. Customers must assess qualification and intended-use requirements.

Can we define our own review rules?

The product is designed around configurable, controlled review criteria. Actual behavior and permissions must be demonstrated and verified in the evaluated build.

Will cloud and on-premises deployment be supported?

Both are potential future options. Availability will depend on technical readiness, security evaluation and deployment qualification.

SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
01 / PRINCIPLES

Controls should be demonstrable.

Access & segregation

Role-based permissions and separation of duties for reviewers, QA decision makers and master-rule owners.

Traceability

Review history and documented rationale to support understanding of decisions.

Controlled configuration

Rule governance, versioning and approvals are design requirements subject to verification.

Data minimization

Planned read-only acquisition from chromatography data systems where feasible and appropriate.

Verification & validation

Risk-based testing and formal validation evidence must precede claims of regulated readiness.

Customer responsibilities

Intended use, SOPs, training, infrastructure and customer qualification require site-specific assessment.
